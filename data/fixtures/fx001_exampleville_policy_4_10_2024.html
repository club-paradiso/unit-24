Section 4.10.4 Procedures

Units shall read back all license plate numbers to the dispatcher using the phonetic alphabet.

Officers shall acknowledge every dispatch with their unit number followed by 10-4.

A request to repeat a transmission shall be made with 10-9; the dispatcher will repeat the entire message.

Vehicle descriptions shall be given in the order color, year, make, model, body style, and license plate.

During mutual aid operations with outside agencies, members shall use plain language and shall not use ten-codes.
